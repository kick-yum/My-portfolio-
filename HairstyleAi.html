Understanding Facial Dynamics

How hair frame proportions, cheekbone angles, and jawlines interact to create visual harmony.

Proportional Balance

Hairstyling relies on creating the illusion of ideal oval symmetry by adding volume to narrow zones or minimizing wider points of the face.

Jawline & Cheekbones

Square and diamond shapes benefit from soft layering to soften sharp angles, while round face shapes benefit from height at the crown.

Texture Interaction

Hair texture dictates how a cut falls naturally. Waves and curls naturally expand horizontally, whereas fine straight hair creates sleek vertical lines.
