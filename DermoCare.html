Knowledge Hub

Common Skin Concerns Quick Guide

Learn how different dermal conditions behave and what evidence-based active ingredients best support recovery.

Acne & Breakouts

Occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil (sebum) and dead skin cells, causing bacterial proliferation (C. acnes).

Best Actives: Salicylic Acid (BHA), Benzoyl Peroxide, Niacinamide, Retinoids.
●

Hyperpigmentation

Post-inflammatory dark spots caused by melanin overproduction following acne breakouts or UV sun damage.

Best Actives: Azelaic Acid, Vitamin C, Alpha Arbutin, Tranexamic Acid, Mineral SPF.

Barrier Dehydration

Damage to the skin's lipid barrier causing moisture loss, flaking, tightness, and increased susceptibility to irritants.

Best Actives: Ceramides, Hyaluronic Acid, Glycerin, Centella Asiatica, Squalane.

Rosacea & Flushing

Vascular hypersensitivity leading to persistent facial flushing, visible blood vessels, and burning sensations.

Best Actives: Azelaic Acid 10%, Colloidal Oatmeal, Panthenol, Allantoin, Zinc Oxide.
